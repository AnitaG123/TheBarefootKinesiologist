Before we begin

"Good luck with me" —
she said, half joking.

Anita has heard this more times than she can count. The woman who arrives laughing about how much she has to work on. Who is half joking — and half genuinely afraid of what might come up.

Here is the truth that changes everything: your body will only ever bring up what you are ready to heal. Not the biggest thing. Not the most frightening thing. The thing that is actually ready to move, right now, in this moment.

The body is not only wise. It is kind. In Anita's experience, the work surfaces only what you are ready to hold, and it unfolds at exactly the pace that is right for you — and only you get to set that pace.

A real session story

"She arrived carrying years of loss, difficult family relationships, and the weight of things she had witnessed growing up. She was braced for the hardest things to surface first.

What came up was body image. How she felt in her own skin that week. The way she was doing everything right and still not feeling at home in herself. It was about self-love — and whether she believed she was worthy of it.

That was what her body was ready for. Not the avalanche. The thing that was actually ready to move."

— Anita, on a client session (shared with permission)

Before you book

You don't need to arrive
ready. You just need to arrive.

Anita has worked with women who came into their first session with years of therapy behind them and still felt afraid. Women who laughed nervously and said "you're going to need more than one session for me." Women who weren't sure they believed any of this would work.

All of them were welcome. All of them were met exactly where they were.

You don't need to know what is wrong. You don't need to have a tidy story prepared. You don't need to be ready to talk about the big things — because your body will decide what is ready to move, and it will begin there. Not with the hardest thing. With the thing that is actually ready.

You also don't need to feel embarrassed about what you are carrying. In every session Anita has held — through her certification training and beyond — nothing has been too much. The women who arrive convinced they are the most complicated case she has ever seen — they never are. They are just human. And that is more than enough to work with.

"The body is not a courtroom. It does not judge what you bring. It simply shows you what is ready — and then it shows you the way through."

— Anita Gibbons · EMK Practitioner

All sessions take place via Zoom or a similar video platform — live or recorded, depending on what works best for you. You will need a quiet space where you feel comfortable and won't be disturbed.

Within kinesiology, EMK is understood to work through the body's energy systems rather than physical touch, so distance is not a barrier to the practice. Anita uses surrogate muscle testing — a long-established approach in kinesiology — using her own body to tune into yours. In her experience, and in the experience of the women she works with, online sessions feel every bit as clear and meaningful as being in the same room.

Before your session Anita will send you everything you need to know so you can arrive feeling settled and prepared.

A live session takes place in real time via video call — you and Anita are present together, and you can ask questions, share what you are experiencing, and have a conversation about what comes up.

A recorded session is conducted by Anita on your behalf and recorded so you can watch it back in your own time. This option suits women in different time zones, those with unpredictable schedules, or anyone who finds it easier to receive the session privately rather than live.

Both are equally effective. The right choice is simply the one that works best for your life.

Each session begins with a conversation. Anita will ask what is present for you right now — what you are carrying, what you would like to work on, what feels most alive or most stuck. You don't need to have an answer. Even "I don't know, I just knew I needed this" is a perfect starting point.

From there, Anita uses muscle monitoring as a biofeedback tool — a way of asking your body directly what it needs and what it is holding. Emotions, beliefs, and patterns that are ready to shift rise to the surface and are released and rebalanced.

Sessions close with Anita bringing your energy back to the present and discussing any integration practices — simple things to support what has been opened — before you go.

Most women describe feeling lighter, clearer, or more settled after a session. Some feel emotional. Some feel surprisingly calm. There is no right way to feel — the body responds in whatever way is right for it.
