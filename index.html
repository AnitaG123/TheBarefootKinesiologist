What is Kinesiology

Your body
holds the answers

Kinesiology is a gentle, non-invasive therapy that uses muscle monitoring as a biofeedback tool — a way of asking your body directly what it needs. It bridges the gap between what the mind believes and what the body actually holds.

Emotions, beliefs, past experiences and energetic blocks all live in the body long after the mind has tried to move on. Kinesiology gives these a voice, allowing them to be acknowledged, processed and released — at the pace that feels right for you.

Anita's approach weaves emotions kinesiology with metaphysical principles, creating a deeply holistic container for women ready to reconnect with their essence and redesign the shape of their lives.

How the unbecoming unfolds

This is not something that happens to you. It is something you choose — and then choose again, session by session, as more of you comes home.

01

You arrive as you are

Your first session is a conversation — spacious, unhurried, and entirely non-judgmental. We explore what brought you here, what your body is carrying, and what you are longing to move toward. No performance required.

Using muscle monitoring as biofeedback, we listen to the body's intelligence. Blocked emotions, outdated beliefs and patterns that were never yours to carry rise to the surface — ready to be released.

As the weight lifts, space opens. Clarity arrives. You begin to recognise the woman you always were beneath the conditioning — and you start building your life from that place. Not a new you. The real one.

Meet Anita

A guide who has
walked the same
shoreline

Anita Gibbons is an Emotions and Metaphysical Kinesiologist based on the Western Australian coast — a practitioner, a woman, and a perpetual student of the ocean.

Her own journey of unbecoming — of peeling back the layers of who she was told to be — is woven into every session she holds. She found kinesiology during a season of her life that required her to completely reimagine who she was, and has never looked back.

Anita's approach is unhurried, deeply attuned, and rooted in the natural world. She believes that healing doesn't happen in a hurry — that like the tide, it has its own perfect rhythm. Her work is a safe, reverent space for women to stop performing and start simply being.

Her own unbecoming

For a long time, Anita's life looked like a series of choices. What it actually was, was a pattern — and beneath it, a belief: everyone leaves anyway. Kinesiology was the turning point. One question in one session — what is the one common denominator in all of your relationships? — was the beginning of everything.

What she put down in that season was not small. And into the space that opened came Macie, a rescue dog from Karratha, and a life that is genuinely, unmistakably hers.
