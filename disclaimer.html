The nature of this work

Emotions & Metaphysical Kinesiology (EMK) is a complementary wellbeing practice. It sits alongside — never in place of — conventional medical and mental-health care. Anita Gibbons is a kinesiology practitioner; she is not a doctor, psychologist, psychiatrist, counsellor, or other registered health professional, and nothing on this website or in a session constitutes medical, psychological, or therapeutic advice.

Kinesiology, muscle testing, and energy-based practices are not supported by the same body of scientific evidence as conventional medicine. What is shared on this site reflects Anita's training, her lived experience, and the experiences women have described to her — it is offered in that spirit, not as a clinical claim.

No diagnosis, treatment, or cure

Anita does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any physical illness, mental-health condition, or disease. EMK sessions are not a substitute for seeing your GP, a specialist, a psychologist, or any other qualified health professional. Please do not stop, start, or change any medication or treatment plan on the basis of anything discussed in a session or read on this site — always speak with your prescribing doctor first.

Your responsibility

By booking a session, you confirm that you are 18 years or older, that you take full responsibility for your own health decisions, and that you understand the complementary nature of this work. Every woman's experience is her own; outcomes described on this website are individual experiences and are not a promise of any particular result for you.

If you need urgent support

EMK is not a crisis service. If you are in distress, experiencing thoughts of harming yourself, or worried about your safety, please reach out for immediate, qualified support:

  • Emergency (Australia): call 000
  • Lifeline: 13 11 14 (24 hours) or lifeline.org.au
  • Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636 (24 hours) or beyondblue.org.au
  • Outside Australia: please contact your local emergency number or crisis line

You matter, and reaching for that kind of support is never a step backwards.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you are unsure whether EMK is right for your circumstances, you are warmly invited to write to Anita at hello@thebarefootkinesiologist.com before booking.
